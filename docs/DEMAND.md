## Provider-side view

Each paid call is a USDC transfer from the MyPadi agent wallet to the provider's
recipient address, settled on Celo and visible on Celoscan. A provider can confirm the
traffic we create independently, by the settlements their recipient address receives;
the per-call settlement tx hashes in `docs/PROOF.md` link straight to those transfers.
